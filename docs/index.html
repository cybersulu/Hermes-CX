Key Features

Built for travelers, remote professionals, shoppers, and engineers who want instant answers without clutter.

💱

Two-Way Currency Calculator

Convert between 110+ world currencies using a built-in numeric keypad. Tap either amount card to calculate forward or in reverse, swap currencies in one tap, and store up to 5 favorite currency pairs for instant switching.

📊

Multi-Currency Compare

Switch to the Compare tab on the converter screen to view your entered amount converted simultaneously across multiple target currencies—ideal for budgeting trips or comparing international prices side by side.

🌍

Live Market Rates Explorer

Browse exchange rates relative to any base currency, search by currency code, name, country, or symbol, and filter across regions including Popular, Europe, Americas, Asia, Middle East, Africa, and Oceania. Pin key currencies to keep them at the top.

📏

Precision & Transfer Fee Estimator

Choose 2, 3, or 4 decimal places for display accuracy. Enable the optional Bank / Transfer Fee slider (0%–10%) to automatically preview fee deductions and your true net received amount.

✈️

Offline Cache & Sync Cadence

Exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API are stored locally in an on-device Room database so conversions continue working offline. Pick how often rates refresh: Manual Only, 15 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours, or Daily, or pull to refresh anytime.

🎨

Material 3 Themes & Custom Colors

Match your style with System, Light, or Dark mode, 7 curated accent palettes, and a built-in custom color picker that generates harmonious light and dark color schemes.

10-Category Unit Converter

Two-way conversions with live conversion formulas, one-tap clipboard copy, and an all-units comparison view.
