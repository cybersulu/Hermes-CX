User Manual

How to Use Hermes CX

Hermes CX is organized into four bottom-navigation tabs: Currency, Market, Units, and Settings. Use this guide to get the most out of every converter, shortcut, and customization option.

1. Currency Tab 2. Multi-Currency Compare 3. Market Tab 4. Units Tab 5. Settings & Themes 6. Offline Mode & Tips
Tab 1 · Currency

Currency Converter & Keypad

Convert between 110+ global currencies in real time. The top card shows the live rate formula (1 TARGET = rate BASE) and when rates were last updated.

Selecting & Swapping Currencies

  1. Tap the top currency capsule (Target / Foreign) or bottom currency capsule (Base) to open the searchable selector sheet.
  2. Search by currency code (e.g., USD, JPY), country, currency name, or symbol, or filter by region.
  3. Tap the circular Swap button between the two rows to invert the Base and Target currencies.

Two-Way Amount Editing

  1. Tap either the top amount box or bottom amount box to choose which side you are typing into.
  2. The active box highlights with “Ready to type”—entering your first digit automatically replaces the existing number so you don’t need to backspace first.
  3. The opposite amount updates immediately as you type.

Using the Numeric Keypad

  • Enter numbers with 0–9 and decimal point ..
  • Tap the Backspace key (DEL) to remove the last digit.
  • Long-press the Backspace key to clear the entire input back to 0.

Favorite Currency Pairs Strip

  • Directly above the keypad, up to 5 favorite currency pairs appear as quick-switch chips.
  • Tap any pair chip to load both currencies immediately.
  • Long-press any pair chip to open the Edit Favorite Pair dialog and customize its From and To currencies.
Refresh Tip: Pull downward anywhere on the Currency screen to immediately fetch the latest market exchange rates.
Tab 1 Sub-View · Compare

Multi-Currency Compare

View your current entered amount converted simultaneously into all of your pinned currencies.

How to Compare Multiple Currencies

  1. On the Currency tab, tap the Compare sub-tab next to Keypad.
  2. Every currency you have pinned (excluding the two currencies currently active in the hero card) appears with its converted total and unit exchange rate.
  3. Tap any currency row in the Compare list to set it as your active counter-currency and return to the keypad.

Populating Your Compare List

The Compare view displays your pinned currencies sorted alphabetically by country. If the list is empty, open the Market tab and tap the pin icon on any currencies you want to track.

Tab 2 · Market

Market Rates Explorer

Scan live exchange rates across regions, pin your most-used currencies, and jump straight into conversions.

Changing the Base Reference

Tap the Base: CODE badge in the top-right corner of the Market screen to choose any base currency. All rows in the list recalculate relative to 1 unit of that base currency.

Searching & Region Filtering

  • Use the search bar to filter by code, currency name, country, or symbol.
  • Tap region chips (All, Popular, Europe, Americas, Asia, Middle East, Africa, Oceania) to narrow the list.

Pinning & Quick Conversion

  • Tap the Pin icon on the right side of any row to pin or unpin that currency. Pinned currencies stay sorted at the top of Market, currency picker sheets, and the Compare tab.
  • Tap anywhere else on a currency card to open it as your Target currency in the Currency tab.
Tab 3 · Units

10-Category Unit Converter

Convert between 80+ everyday and scientific units across 10 measurement categories, complete with live formulas and clipboard copy.

Switching Categories & Units

  • Scroll the horizontal Category Strip above the keypad to switch between Length, Weight & Mass, Temperature, Volume, Area, Speed, Time, Digital Storage, Pressure, and Energy & Power.
  • Tap either unit capsule (top From or bottom To) to pick a different unit in the current category.
  • Tap either amount box for two-way editing, or tap the center Swap button to invert the units.

Formula Banner & Copy

The banner at the top of the card displays the exact conversion formula (for example, 1 m = 3.28084 ft or °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32). Tap Copy to copy the formatted conversion equation to your clipboard.

All-Units Compare View

Switch to the Compare sub-tab on the Units screen to see your current value converted into every unit in the active category at once. Tap any row to make it your Target unit and jump back to the keypad.

Tab 4 · Settings

Preferences, Fees & Themes

Customize how often rates sync, how calculations are formatted, and how Hermes CX looks.

Refresh Frequency & Sync Status

  • Select a sync interval: Manual Only (Save data), Every 15 minutes, Every 1 hour (Recommended), Every 6 hours, or Daily (Every 24 hours).
  • View how many currencies are cached offline, the last-synced timestamp, and your current online/offline status.
  • Tap Sync Market Rates Now at any time for an immediate update.

Decimal Precision & Bank / FX Fee

  • Choose 2, 3, or 4 decimal places for currency result formatting.
  • Toggle Include Bank / Transfer Fee and drag the slider from 0.0% to 10.0% (in 0.5% steps) to preview the fee deducted and net received amount right inside the Currency card.

Theme Mode & Accent Colors

  • Set Display Mode to System, Light, or Dark.
  • Pick from 7 preset palettes (Ocean Blue, Emerald Mint, Royal Violet, Crimson Ruby, Sunset Amber, Nordic Teal, Slate Charcoal) or select Custom and tap Customize Palette to choose any hex color.

In-App Privacy Policy

Scroll to the bottom of Settings and tap the Privacy Policy card to review permissions, local storage details, and third-party API usage inside the app.

Reliability

Offline Mode & Data Privacy

Designed to keep working in airplanes, subways, and low-connectivity travel zones without accounts or tracking.

Automatic Offline Fallback

Every rate sync saves exchange rates locally in an on-device Room database. When no network connection is available, Hermes CX switches automatically to your cached rates and displays an Offline badge alongside the last-updated time. Unit conversions require no internet connection at all.

Quick Navigation & Back Behavior

All preferences—saved pairs, pinned currencies, fee settings, precision, and custom theme colors—are stored locally on your device. Pressing the Android Back button while on Market, Units, or Settings returns you directly to the Currency converter tab.
